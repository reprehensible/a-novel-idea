## Terms and concepts

- **A.S.** — After Silence; scholarly year reckoning. Year 0 = the Silence.
- **the Accord** — the golden age's true name for itself (author-eyes until reveal).
- **Archon** — a ruling deep-graven of the Accord.
- **the Bright Age** — folk name for the golden age.
- **Bright Lords** — mythic demigods of the Bright Age (actually human deep-graven).
- **cairn-line** — Oru-Vael lineage, counted by ancestral cairns.
- **Charter Year (CY)** — Harthen reckoning; CY 1 = A.S. 2363.
- **choir** — many kenners pressing the same letter in drilled unison; modern war-magic.
- **consonance / consonant** — minds shaped to a shared pattern, able to share depth (Accord secret; author-eyes until reveal).
- **the crown's fifth** — Harthen wartime grain requisition share (one part in five; heavier than the prior year's seventh). First: ch 1.
- **assessment mark** — purveyor's chalk cross-in-circle on livestock written down for possible requisition. First: ch 1.
- **the Diapason** — the Accord's master instrument of the Tuning; a held choir of preserved minds; also maintains Ancarel's seals. Folk name: **the Last Bell**.
- **the Discord** — myth: the singular devil that broke the Bright Age. Truth: the rebellion of the discordant, plural.
- **discordant** — Accord term of unpersoning for nonconforming minds; the rebels took it as a badge.
- **First Graving** — the Vireth academies' hushed, licensed graving rite for proven masters.
- **graving / graven** — pressing a kenning permanently into oneself; the resulting state ("thrice-graven").
- **hedge-kenner** — informal village practitioner of a few letters.
- **the Inning** — Harthen harvest-home feast in the moot-barn, first clear evening after the last field comes in. First: ch 1.
- **keeping-letters** — folk name for preserving letters (granary, larder); renewed seasonally when they "go quiet." First: ch 1.
- **the Ken** — the discipline of magic; understanding, enacted.
- **kenner** — a practitioner of the Ken.
- **kenning** — a held, integrated understanding of one narrow aspect of the world, pressable upon it.
- **the Last Bell** — folk/Dawn name for the Diapason.
- **the Ledger of Names** — the Ascendancy's citizenship rolls.
- **letters** — standardized rote kennings; also ordinary literacy (same schooling, same word).
- **the Long Count** — Oru-Vael chanted history; the dead as moral audience.
- **the Low Charter** — Harthen's founding law limiting kenning (graving forbidden).
- **Luminous Precedent** — Vireth official rite venerating exemplary ancestors.
- **memory-debt** — Oru-Vael binding obligation, discharged by deed or telling.
- **Miswrought** — humans warped by partial unraveling around a false graving.
- **pressing** — enacting a kenning on the world. *Pressing to the wick* — spending past warmth into one's own substance; always lethal.
- **quiet roads** — Oru-Vael chant-mapped safe paths through the Scald's fringe.
- **rank-veil** — Vireth face-veil showing examination grade by weave density.
- **salt-oath** — Sarn binding oath sworn on deck and ledger.
- **the Sealing** — the Truce's internment of the old war's survivors in Ancarel.
- **the Silence** — the fall: the severing of the consonance-webs, A.S. 0.
- **the Stillfaith** — Harthen religion: reverence of the shallow and ordinary.
- **the Three Trues** — graving's requirements: true, yours, consistent.
- **the Tithe** — the Accord's annual taking of children for the Tuning (author-eyes until reveal).
- **the Truce** — the mutual internment ending the old war (~A.S. +9).
- **the Tuning** — the Accord's shaping of minds to consonance (author-eyes until reveal).
- **unraveling** — death by false or contradictory graving.
- **warmth** — the bodily reserve spent by pressing; cannot be pressed into oneself.
- **the war road** — Harrowmere's name (older than anyone's memory of why) for the old east road over the Ferrand toll. First: ch 1.
- **the Warden's Latch** — the trustees' key for treating with Ancarel's warden; the story's MacGuffin.
- **"We are quiet."** — Stillfaith grace and its response (in place of amen). First: ch 1.

## Places

- **Ancarel** — the shut vault-city under the Sheer; the Sealing's site. Folk name: **the Shut City**.
- **Fennstead** — the Fenn farm by Harrowmere; water meadows by the mere, fields up the wold's shoulder.
- **Ferrand** — Harrowmere's shire town (moot, levy muster); also the toll on the war road at the wold's shoulder, its light visible from Fennstead. First: ch 1.
- **Marle** — levy training depot south of Ferrand. First: ch 1.
- **Harrowmere** — Joss's home village in the Harthen wolds.
- **Harthen** — Joss's homeland; river-and-wold kingdom under the Low Charter.
- **Lastlight** — prospector outpost on the Scald's rim.
- **the Marches** — contested borderlands between the powers.
- **Oru-Vael** — steppe/high-moor federacy north of the Marches; its people, the Vael.
- **the Scald** — the blighted land where the Silence struck hardest.
- **the Seam** — the war's western front through the Sarn client-belt.
- **the Sheer** — the sea of fused glass over Ancarel.
- **Vell** — free archive-city on the war's seam; falls in Part II.
- **the Vireth Ascendancy** — examination-meritocracy empire; the war's aggressor.
- **the Sarn Concord** — maritime league of city-states; Harthen's treaty partner.

## Organizations and titles

- **Examiner / Examiner-General** — Vireth academic-bureaucratic ranks.
- **the Lector** — Ottavane Dyre's title within the Second Dawn.
- **the Second Dawn** — the secret restorationist society seeking the Diapason.
- **shirelevy** — Harthen's shire-raised soldiery.
- **the Warden** — Ilvane's office under the Truce.

## Phrases, oaths, idioms

- **"Blessed is the hand on the plow."** — Stillfaith creed.
- **"The deeper the carving, the narrower the groove."** — proverb on graving.
- **"Knowing your letters."** — having schooling: literacy and rote magic both.
- **"Pressing to the wick."** — fatal overspending of self; also an idiom for ruinous sacrifice.
