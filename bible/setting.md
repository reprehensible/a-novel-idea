# Setting Bible

## 1. Overview

A single continent of humans, ~3,000 years after **the Silence** — the fall of
a golden age remembered as **the Bright Age**. Magic (**the Ken**) is real,
common at trivial strength, and world-breaking at depths almost no one reaches.
The ruins of the Bright Age litter the land; folk religion holds that the age
of wonders was destroyed by a devil-figure called **the Discord**, and that
reaching too deep into the Ken is the sin that let it in.

Technology is roughly late-medieval-plus: good steel, watermills, printing in
the cities, ocean-going ships — with rote magic filling the gaps (preserving,
mending, kindling, signaling). No gunpowder; letter-choirs are artillery.

A continental war is two years old: the **Vireth Ascendancy** is grinding west
into the client-kingdoms of the **Sarn Concord**, and the Kingdom of
**Harthen** has been dragged in by treaty. The war is the engine and the cover
for everything the story's antagonists do.

**Tone of the world:** lived-in, not grimdark. Harvests still matter, guilds
still bicker, the terror of the deep past is background noise — until it
isn't.

## 2. The Ken (magic system) — HARD RULES

*The rules below are fixed. Nothing in any climax may violate them. Additions
allowed; contradictions are bugs.*

The world yields to minds that truly comprehend the thing being changed.
Magic is understanding, enacted.

1. **Kennings.** A *kenning* is a held, integrated understanding of one narrow
   aspect of the world (fire-into-heat; how water finds its level; the
   patience of soil). Understandings are partly metaphorical, because human
   minds are — but the metaphor must be load-bearing and true *for that mind*.
2. **Pressing.** Enacting a kenning on the world is called *pressing*. Words,
   glyphs, and gestures are mnemonic scaffolding only — they hold the mind in
   shape; they have no power in themselves.
3. **Cost: warmth.** Pressing draws the practitioner's bodily warmth, scaling
   with (magnitude × the world's resistance ÷ depth of understanding).
   Overspending brings shivering, frost on the skin and clothes, frostbite
   from within, death. Warmth returns slowly with rest, food, fire. **You
   cannot press warmth into yourself** — no engine feeds itself.
   - *Pressing to the wick:* it is possible to spend past warmth into one's
     own substance. Always lethal. Known, whispered, almost never done.
4. **Letters.** Standardized rote kennings — borrowed, shallow copies of some
   dead master's understanding, drilled via glyph-and-chant. Weak but
   reliable and identical for everyone (hence guild licensing and testing).
   "Knowing your letters" means exactly this; common literacy and common
   magic are the same schooling. Perhaps 1 in 5 people can hold a few
   letters; 1 in 400 reaches journeyman; 1 in 10,000 becomes a master with
   any personal kenning at all.
5. **Depth is personal.** Past journeyman, progress means making
   understanding *yours* — idiosyncratic, non-transferable. Masters teach
   methods of study, never content. Two masters' fire-kennings are not
   interchangeable and can actively interfere. This is why there are no
   academies of deep magic, why the strong are solitary, and why power
   cannot simply be trained into armies.
6. **Choirs.** Many minds pressing the *same letter* in drilled unison
   multiply effect sublinearly (a hundred choristers ≈ ten masters). The
   deeper an individual, the worse they choir — individuality is dissonance.
   Modern war-magic is letter-choirs: grinding, logistical, artillery-like.
7. **Graving.** Pressing a kenning permanently *into oneself* — restructuring
   body and mind according to one's understanding. Graving is how humans
   exceed human limits, and it compounds: each graving multiplies capability.
   The **three trues** (canonical requirements):
   - **True:** the understanding must survive the world's audit. A false
     understanding graved in = *unraveling* (death), or, if partial, the
     **Miswrought**.
   - **Yours:** built from your own interior. Borrowed understanding simply
     fails to take (this fail-safe is why the rote-minded cannot grave, and
     why power gates on introspection).
   - **Consistent:** with every prior graving and with what you actually are.
     Contradiction between gravings = unraveling. Each graving therefore
     narrows all future ones: *the deeper the carving, the narrower the
     groove.* The mighty are inflexible in characteristic ways; every great
     power is a single theory of self, made flesh, unable to be otherwise.
8. **What graving gives.** Per the understanding graved: endurance, expanded
   warmth-reserves, senses, self-repair. The graven age at the rate their
   self-understanding dictates — the deep-graven endure centuries. A person's
   gravings are countable and known of ("thrice-graven"); more than three is
   rumor in the modern age. Nobody living is known past four.
9. **The Miswrought.** Partial unravelings: humans warped around a false
   understanding. Range from pitiable to titanic. They drift toward the
   Scald, where the land agrees with them.
10. **Sealing.** A stasis-graving pressed onto *another* is near-impossible —
    you cannot deeply ken another mind — except by consonance-mediated
    working (see §5). This rule matters at the climax and must not be broken
    casually anywhere else.

**Modern institutional shape:** village *hedge-kenners* (a few letters);
guild-licensed journeymen and masters in towns; Vireth's academies (see §3);
army letter-choirs. The deep-graven of the present age: a few dozen known
names on the continent, mostly solitary, mostly strange.

## 3. Nations and factions (present day)

### Kingdom of Harthen
Joss's homeland. River-and-wold country: turf-roofed longbarns, orchards and
mead, boundary-walking rites at year's turn, a culture of understatement and
practical competence. Ruled by a Chartered king constrained by the **Low
Charter** (A.S. 2363) and a moot of guilds and shires. The Charter *limits
kenning by law*: letters free to all, mastery licensed, graving forbidden —
folk memory says reaching broke the world once. Religion: the **Stillfaith**
— reverence of the shallow and ordinary; "blessed is the hand on the plow."
At war reluctantly, by treaty with Sarn; levies raised by shire.

### The Vireth Ascendancy
The continent's rising power, east and south. An examination-meritocracy:
rank earned by tiered examinations, displayed in **rank-veils** (sheer face
veils, weave density by grade); house-name first, *sen* = "of house."
Institutions: the **Ledger of Names** (citizenship rolls), state kenning
academies with standardized curricula — which produce the best letter-choirs
in the world (they believe it is drill; see §5.4). The academies quietly
operate a licensed **First Graving** rite for proven masters — some die; the
cost is hushed and accepted. Official rite venerates the **Luminous
Precedent**: exemplary ancestors, not gods. The war's stated cause: security
and unification; its real engine: an elite surplus of examined men and women
hungry for posts, and (secretly, for a few) the Second Dawn's purposes.

### The Sarn Concord
A maritime league of city-states west and north: ship-clans, salt-oaths sworn
on deck and ledger, coin-braids worn in the hair to show standing. No gods —
contracts. Harthen's treaty partner; losing the war's western seam.

### Oru-Vael
Steppe and high-moor federacy north of the Marches. Lineages counted in
**cairn-lines**; the honored dead recited in the **Long Count**, a chanted
history that is also their religion — the dead as moral audience. They keep
the **quiet roads**: chant-mapped safe paths through the Scald's fringe.
Communal rote-circles; graving is absolute taboo. Memory-debts are their
binding social currency.

### The Marches and the Scald
Between the powers: the Marches, contested and emptying. Beyond: **the
Scald** — the land the old war burned, where the Silence struck hardest.
Blighted, cold-zoned, roamed by Miswrought, dotted with ruins and
prospector camps. At its heart, under a sea of fused glass called **the
Sheer**, lies the shut city of **Ancarel** (§5.6).

### The Second Dawn
A secret restorationist society, decades old, centered in the Ascendancy's
archives and high examiners but transnational in membership. They know a
*partial* truth: the Bright Ones were human kenners, not gods, and their age
can be rebuilt. They idealize what they cannot fully see. Their project: a
generations-long archival hunt that has located Ancarel and identified the
weapon within (**the Diapason**, folk-named *the Last Bell*). Their plan: win
the war in one stroke and use the peace to restore the golden age. Leader in
the field: the **Lector**, Ottavane Dyre. They are sincere. That is the
problem.

## 4. Religion, myth, and the shape of the past (AS PUBLICLY BELIEVED)

- **The Bright Age:** demigod **Bright Lords** raised the Standing Cities,
  roads that carried you, lights hung in the sky. Paradise.
- **The Discord:** a devil, singular — envy given a crown — who corrupted men
  and brought the age down in fire.
- **The Silence:** the moment the Bright Age's song stopped. Then the **Quiet
  Centuries**: plague, dark, and forgetting; the current nations rose from
  the wreckage.
- **Moral:** do not reach above your station; depth is hubris; the shallow
  are blessed. (The Stillfaith's creed; Harthen's Low Charter; Oru-Vael's
  graving taboo — the whole present order is built on this lesson.)
- Ruins are cursed and shunned; prospectors who work them are despised and
  indispensable.

## 5. THE TRUTH (author-eyes canon; staged reveals)

### 5.1 Layer T1 — the golden age was a tyranny *(revealed ~ch 41)*
The Bright Age called itself **the Accord**. Its rulers, the **Archons**, were
deep-graven humans — the mightiest ever, because the Accord had industrialized
depth. Its engine and its crime was **the Tuning**: minds shaped from
childhood to a shared pattern (*consonance*) can share deep understanding and
choir superlinearly — the one workaround to the individuality limit. So the
Accord standardized human minds: the annual **Tithe** of children, graded
castes of permitted understanding, the unpersoning of *discordant* minds.
Wonders above, funded by the theft of interiority below. Accord archives are
serene about this; they were proud of it.

### 5.2 Layer T2 — the dark enemy was a rebellion, and it went sour *(revealed ~ch 51–55)*
**The Discord** was not a devil but a movement — the discordant, plural: wild,
individual understanding; reckless self-graving without the Accord's
scaffolding (producing both their heroes and legions of Miswrought). They won.
And then: tribunals, purges of "crowned minds," each committee devouring the
last — the revolution ate itself. The Silence was their final weapon: a
working that severed the Accord's consonance-webs — mass death and catatonia
of the tuned, collapse of every maintained wonder. The myth's single devil is
history's compression of a mass movement; the myth's moral is Accord
propaganda fossilized in trauma.

### 5.3 The Truce and the Sealing *(revealed ~ch 55–66)*
The war ended not in victory but exhaustion: surviving Archons, Unbound
captains of the Discord, and the worst Miswrought titans were sealed in
**Ancarel**, the Accord's final redoubt, by mutual internment — **the
Truce**. The sealing was only possible using the enemy's engine (§5.5). The
Truce's broker and volunteer warden: **Ilvane** (see her file). Outside
trustees held the **Warden's Latch**, a key to treat with the warden; the
trustee lineage failed centuries ago and the Latch was lost in a minor ruin —
until the Dawn's survey found it, and one surveyor, understanding what it
meant, fled with it. That flight opens our story.

### 5.4 Consonance in the present
The Ascendancy's standardized academies are unknowingly re-deriving weak
consonance — their curricula produce unusually compatible minds, hence their
superior choirs. They think it is drill. The Second Dawn's inner circle knows
exactly what it is, and wants the real thing.

### 5.5 The Diapason *(true nature revealed ~ch 60–66)*
The Accord's master instrument of the Tuning: not a machine but a held choir —
a bell whose ringing is ten thousand preserved consonant minds. Capabilities:
broadcast tuning (impose patterns on minds in range — rote patterns
instantly, depth with exposure), consonance-brokering, and — since the Truce —
**maintenance of every seal in Ancarel**. The rebels who won used the thing
they had fought; the Truce runs on the tyranny's engine. Therefore: *the
weapon and the seals are the same object.* Deny it, break it, and everything
sealed wakes. There is no clean option. Range: city-scale direct; anything
larger stays vague (do not commit).

### 5.6 Ancarel
The shut city under the Sheer: intact, lit, waiting; galleries of hundreds of
sealed sleepers of three kinds (Archons and Accord remnants; Unbound; the
titan Miswrought). Its doors answer the Warden's Latch, or force. Interior
detail is decided when drafting Part IV–V, and recorded here as it lands.

## 7. Reference details

- **Money:** Harthen: pennies/shillings struck by the Crown-and-Moot. Vireth:
  graded scrip against the Ledger. Sarn: weighed salt-marked coin. (Keep
  loose; pin amounts in glossary as used.)
- **Languages:** a common trade tongue (the prose's "translation"); Old
  Accord speech survives in ruins and scholarship (*Ancarel, Ilvane, sen-*).
- **Calendar:** scholars reckon **A.S.** (After Silence); Harthen uses
  Charter Years (CY 611 = A.S. 2974, the story's start). Seasons as Earth's.
- **The sky:** normal, except over the deep Scald, where the stars are wrong
  (a scar of the Silence; do not explain further in book 1).
