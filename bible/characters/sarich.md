# Sarich — "the Cold Man"

**Role:** Major antagonist; the Dawn's hunter; pursuit engine of Parts I–IV
**First appears:** ch 6 (as the "merchant"; named ch 9)

## Essentials
40s. One name — he gave up his house name with everything else. Weathered,
unremarkable, forgettable on purpose; dresses too lightly for every
weather. Once-graven, and the graving is the character:

**The graving:** crossing a frozen pass with his young daughter, both
dying, he graved out his fear — and his understanding of fear was
entangled with everything warm in him, so the consistency web took it all:
he does not feel cold, fear, or warmth of any kind, bodily or otherwise
(canon demonstration: a graving takes what your understanding *actually*
contains, not what you meant). His daughter lived. She is grown, in Sarn
territory, and does not know him; he has watched her from streets away.
Revealed in fragments (ch 49 primarily, via Cael).

## Voice
Flat, brief, precise; no cruelty and no mercy — costs and outcomes. Asks
unsettlingly sincere questions about what things feel like.

Sample lines:
- "You'll run now. It adds a day."
- "Was it warm, doing that? Describe it."

## The Ken
Hunter's kennings: track, silence, patience, the unhurried step that eats
miles. Immune to the Scald's cold-zones (his one great advantage there).
In a fight: economical, sudden, done. Cannot be baited, tired, or
frightened — must be out-thought or out-*felt*.

## Function and arc
The pursuit constant: Harrowmere (ch 6–9), Vell (ch 21, 27), the tarn
(ch 39), the leash on Cael (ch 45–52), the thief's way into Ancarel
(ch 59), the last confrontation (ch 61). His narrowness is his limit: he
cannot ken what he cannot feel, and the endgame turns on things felt.
**Resolution (ch 61):** beaten not by force — Renna's answer and Joss's
offered human warmth reach whatever audited the graving; he stands down,
and walks into the vault's deeps, ambiguous, a sequel thread. Log any
change.

## Relationships
- **The Lector:** the only person he serves; she is honest with him.
- **Renna:** recognizes her as his mirror-in-reverse; their duel-of-craft
  spans three acts.
- **Cael:** his leashed charge; Sarich lets the warning slip — deniably —
  in ch 45 (canon: he chose to).
- **The daughter:** never named on page in book 1; sequel material.
