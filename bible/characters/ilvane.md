# Ilvane — the Warden

**Role:** Major (Parts IV–V); the Truce's broker and Ancarel's keeper
**First appears:** ch 55 (dream-contact); in person ch 58

## Essentials
Ancient — Truce-era, held by the seal-stasis she administers. Accord-raised
consonant who defected to the Discord early, saw the Terror from inside,
and brokered the Truce: *the only one both sides trusted to hold the door,
because she had betrayed both.* Deep-graven beyond modern reckoning
(uncounted; do not number her gravings on page in book 1). Appears as a
plain woman of no fixable age in warden's grey; the city lights follow her
attention.

## Voice
Old-fashioned formal diction with sudden colloquial drops (she has been
listening to prospectors' chatter through the stones for centuries and it
shows). Loneliness managed as courtesy. Never lies; curates truth in
layers — her half-truths (ch 55) are tests, not malice.

Sample lines:
- "You have questions. I have three thousand years of answers and no one
  has liked any of them."
- "We sealed ourselves because we could not stop. Write that down. No one
  ever writes that down."

## Knowledge (the exposition keystone — release on schedule)
Sole living holder of the full truth: T1 (the Tuning, the Tithe), T2 (the
Discord as movement; the Terror), the Truce, and §5.5 (weapon = seals).
Reveal order: hints and tests (ch 55, 57) → Truce's failure and the
dilemma (ch 60, 66) → full testimony threaded through Part V. She must
never infodump ahead of the outline's reveal schedule.

## Wants
To be relieved. To have the Truce *matter* — three thousand years of
holding a door everyone forgot. She is not neutral: she will accept the
seals breaking over the Diapason ringing again, and says so plainly when
finally asked plainly (ch 66). Whether her long game runs deeper than she
shows: unresolved, deliberately (sequels).

## Function and arc
Gatekeeper → tester (the gate's price, ch 57) → counselor with an agenda
(ch 60, 66) → holds the corridor in the cascade (ch 72) → departs "to
count who woke" with a warning (ch 75). Ambiguous ally; the sequels'
hinge-character.

## Under pressure
Does arithmetic aloud — lives, years, costs — then acts without hurry.
Her power on page stays *implied* in book 1 (holding, warding, the city's
attention); no full display. Log any change.

## Relationships
- **Joss:** the first mind in millennia the gate read clean; she finds
  him restful and says so, which unnerves everyone.
- **Serel:** recognizes what Serel is carrying (unforgetting + guilt);
  gives her the truth gently. Witnesses the wick.
- **The Lector:** regards her the way one regards a child playing with a
  found detonator — pity, horror, and no patience.
- **The sleepers:** she knows every name. She sealed friends on both
  sides. Do not spend this lightly.
