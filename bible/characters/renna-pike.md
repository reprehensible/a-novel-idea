# Renna Pike

**Role:** Major; companion; love interest; occasional POV (1–2 chapters)
**First appears:** ch 3

## Essentials
22. Poacher's daughter from the Harthen fells; shirelevy scout billeted at
Harrowmere for the requisitions. Lean, quick, weathered; dark hair in a
tail; a crossbow she maintains like a religion; whittles when idle. Reads
slowly, tracks brilliantly.

## Voice
Flint and economy. Jokes delivered deadpan at the worst moment; swears by
poacher's oaths ("by the warden's blind eye"). Says true things bluntly and
kind things almost never aloud — shows care by provisioning people.

Sample lines:
- "Dead men are heavy. Walk faster."
- "I like him fine. Shut up."

## Wants and fears
- Surface want: to do the job in front of her.
- Deep want: to be somewhere she chose, with people she chose — she has
  never once chosen; life happened to her.
- Fear: exposure of her secret; being sent back.
- **Secret (exposed at the gate, ch 57):** she deserted her levy post to
  follow Joss out of the burning of Harrowmere — a hanging offense in
  Harthen. The "brave one" has been afraid the whole time. Resolution: the
  company is what she chose; she says so plainly, and it holds.

## The Ken
Barely lettered — a game-snare letter, a fire-letter, that's all, and she
distrusts the rest. Her competence is entirely mundane, and the story
honors it: scouting, shooting, cold-craft. She is the proof the book needs
that the unmagical are not extras.

## Arc
Duty → desertion (secret) → chosen loyalty declared → full partner in the
denial at Ancarel. Counter-hunter to Sarich across the middle acts (ch 21,
27, 61): his coldness against her warmth, literally and figuratively.

## How she fights
Ambush, angles, economy. One bolt where it ends the problem. Hates fair
fights on principle. Carries the party through the Scald's cold-zones —
her craft, not magic, is what beats the cold.

## Relationships
- **Joss:** see his file; she'd deny "love interest," then marry him.
- **Serel:** mutual respect conducted entirely through argument.
- **Sarich:** her opposite number; she is the one who finally answers him
  (ch 61).
- **Tovan:** road-siblings; teach each other whistling signals / knots.
