# Josselin "Joss" Fenn

**Role:** Protagonist; primary POV (~60% of chapters)
**First appears:** ch 1

## Essentials
19 at story start. Third child of Bram and Lise Fenn of Fennstead, by
Harrowmere in the Harthen wolds. Middle height, farm-built; unruly brown
hair he hacks short himself; a burn scar across the left palm (childhood,
kettle); habitually turns things over in his hands while thinking. Elder
brother Davin (conscripted ch 1), younger sister Petra (14).

## Voice
Wold cadences: understatement, concrete nouns, dry mild humor ("Well.
That's the barn gone."). Asks real questions and listens to the answers —
his defining habit. As POV: notices work, weather, soil, animals, what
things cost; metaphors from farming. Not bookish, not stupid: patient.

Sample lines:
- "I don't know what it is. That's why I'm asking."
- "You can't lie to a field. It comes up what you put down."

## Wants and fears
- Surface want: to get his family through the war intact.
- Deep want: to *matter* — he'd deny it, but the vanity is real, and the
  gate at Ancarel will make him say it aloud (ch 57).
- Fear: that Wystan died for nothing; later, that wanting to matter makes
  him the same as the Dawn.
- The lie he believes at start: that curiosity is a small, safe thing.

## The Ken
Ordinary talent — middling with letters, no prodigy. His edge is
**unusually honest introspection**: he doesn't flinch from true things
about himself, which is the actual gate to depth. Progression (book 1):
- Letters under Wystan (baseline, ch 2).
- First personal kenning under Serel: **the patience of soil** — settling,
  holding, steadying; firming ground, calming beasts. Modest, rural,
  his (ch 19). Used cleverly, never as artillery (e.g., ch 50).
- Climax: **first graving — rootedness** ("I know what I am and where I
  stand"): he cannot be re-tuned; external kennings grip him poorly. Not a
  combat power; it is why he can walk through the Grand Tuning (ch 67, 70).
  The graving nearly unravels on his dodged guilt over Wystan; he passes by
  accepting it as true.

## Arc
Bystander who helps a dying stranger → courier by accident → student →
witness (the archive, ch 41: purpose re-founded from "protect my own" to
"no one gets to own minds") → the one mind the Bell can't grip. Ends
once-graven, changed, no longer able to go home in the old sense — and
choosing the door anyway.

## How he acts under pressure
Steadies. Gets slower and simpler when others get fast. Protects first,
wins later. Fights like a farmer: leverage, tools, terrain, patience — never
flash.

## Relationships
- **Renna Pike:** friction → trust → slow-burn love; she moves, he holds;
  by Part V they are a settled fact neither has made speeches about.
- **Serel Vaun:** wary student → real trust broken (ch 24) and rebuilt
  harder; her death (ch 69) is the price of his walk.
- **Cael:** mirror — what Joss might be if his wanting-to-matter had found
  an institution. Wary → genuine.
- **Wystan Crake:** first teacher; his death (ch 7) is the engine of Joss's
  guilt and the near-fatal knot of his graving.
- **Family:** alive, displaced to kin at Millbrook after Fennstead burns;
  letters intermittent; homecoming coda ch 76.
