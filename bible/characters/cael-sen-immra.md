# Cael sen Immra

**Role:** Major; secondary POV (~20%); Dawn initiate → defector
**First appears:** ch 10 (his POV thread)

## Essentials
24. Vireth, house Immra (minor, proud, indebted — his examination success
is the family's one asset). Archivist-grade; fourth-tier rank-veil.
Slight, tidy, quick-handed with documents; owns exactly one luxury, good
ink. Keeps a private journal in a cipher of his own design — it becomes
evidence (ch 36 → 53) and testimony (ch 74).

## Voice
Educated, earnest, a beat too formal in speech; thinks in lists and
citations; self-mocking in the journal where no one sees. His POV chapters
render the Dawn from inside: reasonable voices, immaculate paperwork,
atrocity as agenda item.

Sample lines:
- "The precedent is clear. The precedent is the problem."
- (journal) "Today we did a necessary thing. I notice I keep writing that."

## Wants and fears
- Surface want: to be part of something that ends the war and mends the
  world — the Dawn's promise.
- Deep want: to be *good*, and to be seen to be good — he joined for the
  first and stayed too long for the second.
- **Truth exposed at the gate (ch 57):** he still, in his marrow, wants
  the utopia. Honesty about that — not renunciation — is what passes him.
- Fear: that his family's standing dies with his defection (it will; he
  goes anyway).

## The Ken
Competent letters, archive-craft kennings (preservation, reading damaged
text). No combat value. His weapons are memory, documents, and the truth.
Resists the Grand Tuning (ch 68) by anchoring on his journal's recorded
truths — the honest record as a self he can hold onto (system-consistent:
self-knowledge resists tuning).

## Arc
True believer → cracks (interrogations, ch 22) → break (the tuned village,
ch 36; he starts the true journal) → attempted desertion, caught, leashed
(ch 45, but his warning gets out) → real defection at the refuge atrocity
(ch 52) → joins the company (ch 53) → witness under the Bell (ch 68) →
carries the testimony out (ch 74): from archivist of lies to witness.
His arc is the rebellion theme in miniature — idealism, complicity, break —
run to a better ending than the Discord managed.

## Under pressure
Freezes, then files: fear processed as procedure. Physical courage is
absent and moral courage arrives late and absolute.

## Relationships
- **The Lector:** his sponsor and idol; watching her grave out her doubt
  (ch 42) is the beginning of his end with the Dawn.
- **Serel:** his old examiner — the defector who left the door in his mind
  open. Their reunion (ch 53) is the hinge of Part IV's trust plot.
- **Joss:** mirror (see Joss's file); Joss's plain questions do more work
  on him than any argument.
- **Sarich:** his leash (ch 45–52); mutual, unwilling understanding.
