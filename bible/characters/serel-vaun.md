# Serel Vaun

**Role:** Major; mentor; POV (~10%)
**First appears:** ch 8 (shadowing from ch 4)

## Essentials
Mid-50s. Born **Serel sen Davra**, Vireth; rose to Examiner at the capital
academies; "Vaun" is a taken scholar-name from a dead language she loves.
Grey-streaked hair severely pinned; ink-stained fingers; carries a
plain traveling slate-book of her own devising. Twice-graven: (1)
**unforgetting** (the academy's standard First Graving for examiners —
perfect recall), (2) **the still hand** (perfect fine control; surgical
pressing precision). Frost-scarred along both forearms after ch 27.

## Voice
Precise, dry, interrogative — teaches by question. Contempt for sloppiness;
tenderness expressed as rigor. Occasionally, rarely, funny on purpose.

Sample lines:
- "Wrong. Usefully wrong. Say it again and listen to yourself."
- "I remember everything. It is not the gift you imagine."

## Wants and fears
- Surface want: to stop the Second Dawn from reaching Ancarel.
- Deep want: absolution she does not believe she deserves.
- **Secret (staged: identity exposed ch 24; the whole truth at the gate,
  ch 57):** she is not merely a defector — she was a *founder* of the
  Dawn's archival project in her youth. The triangulation of Ancarel is
  built on her methods. She fled when the inner circle's aims clarified;
  she has been trying to out-run her own life's work since.
- Fear: that the Dawn is her true legacy; that unforgetting means no
  forgiveness is ever possible, because nothing fades.

## The Ken
The strongest ally on the page until Part V — and deliberately limited:
deep in *record, structure, precision* (her kennings run to glass, ink,
stillness, edges), not battle-magic. Her duels (ch 27, 69) are won or
survived by exactness under cost, shown with hard rules: she spends warmth
visibly, stops short of the wick in ch 27 **and explains what the wick is**
(Chekhov). Teaches Joss method, never content; articulates the Three Trues
(ch 35).

## Arc
Watcher → reluctant teacher → exposed → trusted again on truer terms →
the whole confession → chooses the wick at the Diapason (ch 69), buying
Joss's walk with everything, unforgetting at last. Her death is the
mentor-price of the climax and must land as chosen, not spent by the plot.

## Under pressure
Goes quiet and exact. Triage-minded; will amputate (plans, positions,
her own standing) without ceremony. The still hand never shakes; the
person does, after.

## Relationships
- **Joss:** the student she never had; she is retraining her own habit of
  shaping people (examiner's reflex) into actual teaching.
- **Cael:** her former student at the academy — his defection lands on her
  as both vindication and indictment (ch 53).
- **Ottavane Dyre:** old colleagues; the Lector still calls her by her
  birth name. Their duel is also an argument they've been having for
  thirty years.
- **Renna:** respect via argument; Serel leaves her the slate-book.
