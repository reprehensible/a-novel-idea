# Novel Outline — Book One (working title: *The Warden's Latch*)

> The CURRENT plan. Deviations get logged in `notes/decisions-log.md` and
> propagated here. Beats for Parts IV–V are one refinement pass coarser than
> Parts I–II by design; each part gets a beat-tightening pass as drafting
> approaches it (log those passes too).

## Logline

A farmboy who helps a dying stranger inherits the key to a sealed city of
the old world — and must reach it before the secret society that wants to
end a war by restoring the tyranny that once broke the world.

## Themes

Understanding as power → honesty with self as the only safe foundation.
Order and individuality as twin failures when either devours the other.
History as propaganda; revolutions and their teeth. The unmagical mattering.

## POV plan

Joss ~60% · Cael ~20% · Serel ~10% · specials (Renna ×2, rare antagonist
beats inside Cael's thread) · 4 epistolary interludes. No other POVs
without a logged decision.

## Structure and budget

Five parts, 76 chapters + 4 interludes ≈ **292k words** (chapters avg
~3,800; interludes 600–1,200).

| Part | Ch | Words | Question it opens | State change it delivers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I — The Wold | 1–14 | ~52k | What did the dying man carry? | Home lost; the hunt begun; trapped in Vell |
| II — The Seam | 15–30 | ~60k | What is the case, and who wants it? | The mission crystallized; Vell fallen; north |
| III — The Long Road | 31–46 | ~60k | What actually fell, three thousand years ago? | Twist T1; purpose re-founded; the Scald's edge |
| IV — The Scald | 47–62 | ~60k | Can they reach the door first — and what is behind it? | Twist T2; the company whole; both sides inside |
| V — The Last Bell | 63–76 | ~55k | What does denial cost? | Weapon denied; seals broken; the world's frame changed |

## Part I — The Wold (ch 1–14)

### Ch 1 — Harvest  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- Fennstead at harvest; requisition officers in Harrowmere; wold life and
  rote magic as texture (preserving-letters on the granary, Wystan blessing
  boundary stones).
- Family established; Davin's conscription notice arrives mid-feast.
- Ends on: distant smoke on the war road, watched from the barn roof.

### Ch 2 — Letters  [POV: Joss] [~3,500]
- Village letters lesson under Wystan: the Ken's rote tier on page (rules 2,
  3, 4 shown small); Joss middling but *asks the right questions*.
- Refugee family from the Seam billeted at the mill; first war-stories.
- Ends on: Wystan, watching Joss: "You listen wrong for a farmer." (plant)

### Ch 3 — The Levy Scout  [POV: Joss] [~3,600]
- Renna Pike introduced: billeted scout, requisition friction, a poacher's
  bet won against the village's best; prickly first meeting with Joss.
- Requisition escalates: the Fenns' second horse taken; Bram's silence.
- Ends on: Renna, off-hand: the Seam is worse than the couriers say.

### Ch 4 — The Man on the Wold  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- Storm; Joss finds Adlen Marsh half-dead on the high wold — frost-wounds
  in summer (clue: overspending). Carries him down to Wystan.
- Strange effects around the man: ground firmed where he lay (his last
  pressings). Wystan grim for the first time.
- Ends on: Marsh's grip on Joss's wrist: "Not the guild. No one."

### Ch 5 — What the Dying Carry  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Marsh's delirium: fragments — *Ancarel*, *the Last Bell*, "they are not
  wrong about the wonder." Dies at dawn. The sealed case remains.
- Wystan, torn, sends guild-word upriver anyway (his one mistake).
- Ends on: Joss alone with the case, turning it over in his hands.

### Ch 6 — The Merchant  [POV: Joss] [~3,500]
- A "merchant caravan" arrives asking gentle questions: Sarich, unnamed,
  wrongly dressed for the cold evening. Dread chapter; nothing happens.
- Renna marks him instantly ("he walks like the ground owes him").
- Ends on: the merchant buying Wystan a drink.

### Ch 7 — Fire at Fennstead  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Night raid for the case: Fennstead fired as diversion; Wystan dies
  shielding Joss (his one good battle-letter, held too long); Bram wounded.
- Renna kills two raiders; Joss keeps the case by dumb stubbornness.
- Ends on: the barn burning; Wystan's chalk still on Joss's sleeve.

### Ch 8 — The Woman Who Watched  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Aftermath; family to kin at Millbrook; guilt laid in (the guild-word —
  was it Joss's find that killed Wystan? — the knot for ch 67).
- Serel reveals herself: she tracked Marsh too. Hard truths: "they will
  not stop." Bram gives Joss the good knife; leaving as its own grief.
- Ends on: three of them on the north road before light. (Renna's
  desertion happens here, unremarked — plant, pays ch 57.)

### Ch 9 — The Ferry  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]
- First pursuit set piece: the river ferry; Sarich named; his flat
  economy of violence shown; escape by Renna's angles and Joss firming a
  mudbank (first hint his farm-sense touches the Ken).
- Serel presses in earnest for the first time — cost shown: frost on her
  sleeves, her hands steady, her lips grey.
- Ends on: Sarich on the far bank, not hurrying.

### Ch 10 — The Lector's Table  [POV: Cael] [~3,600]
- Thread opens inside the Dawn: the archive-crypt under the academy; the
  Lector's sermon-brief — the war ended in one stroke, the age rebuilt;
  sincere, luminous, appalling only in hindsight.
- Cael assigned to retrieval logistics; his cipher journal introduced.
- Ends on: the map table — a pin in the Scald; a pin near Harrowmere.

### Ch 11 — Method  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Road school: why letters are weak, what depth is (rules 4, 5 taught
  through a task — Joss fails usefully); Serel's question-teaching.
- Renna–Serel friction sets the triangle's temperature.
- Ends on: the case will not open to force or letters: they need the
  Glassine Hall at Vell.

### Ch 12 — Guild-Word  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- River town: the guild contact is dead — the word Wystan sent got him
  killed; the Dawn is ahead of them as well as behind (scope dread).
- Narrow escape via undercroft; first ruin-stones seen (Bright Age masonry
  under a granary — the past is *load-bearing*).
- Ends on: wanted-word out on three travelers; roads closing.

### Ch 13 — The Seam  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Crossing the war's edge: a battlefield a week old. Choir-craters; frost
  where no frost should be; the levy dead lying in Harthen colors.
- No glory; Renna's silence; Joss understands what Davin marched into.
- Ends on: Vell's towers over the plain — and siege-lines being dug.

### Ch 14 — Gates Closing  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Into Vell as the Ascendancy's "protection" negotiations collapse;
  refugee crush; the gates shut behind them.
- City texture: archive-city, neutral and doomed, everything for sale.
- Ends on: across the square, buying bread — the merchant who doesn't
  feel cold. Trapped in with the hunter. **[Part I ends]**

### Interlude A — "The Lamps of the Bright Age" (epistolary, ~900)
- A Stillfaith homily as told at Harrowmere's hearths: the myth entire —
  Bright Lords, the singular Discord, the Silence, the moral. (The reader
  holds the official story before the book starts breaking it.)

## Part II — The Seam (ch 15–30)

### Ch 15 — Vell  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Lodging in the Ropewalk district; the Glassine Hall's bureaucracy —
  comedy with menace under it; a fixer engaged: Mott (glossary on
  drafting).
- Renna maps bolt-holes; Serel is *known* here and hides it badly (plant → 24).
- Ends on: an appointment slip, ten days out. The siege won't wait.

### Ch 16 — The Case Opens  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Serel's methods + the Hall's lens: the case opens: the **Warden's
  Latch** (a keying stone that is *cold*), and Marsh's journal.
- First journal excerpts: the survey, the balk, "wrong about the price."
  They know the destination now: a city under the Scald.
- Ends on: Serel: "I know who is coming for this. Better than anyone
  alive." (She doesn't say why. Plant → 24, 57.)

### Ch 17 — Diplomatic Cover  [POV: Cael] [~3,600]
- Dawn advance team enters Vell under diplomatic cover; Cael's first
  fieldwork; Sarich attached (the leash-pairing begins).
- The Lector's brief, re-read in Cael's hand: the Latch matters more than
  the city. First near-miss with Joss (a queue, unnoticed).
- Ends on: siege guns of the choir-lines tuning up beyond the walls.

### Ch 18 — The Walls  [POV: Joss] [~4,100]
- Siege begins: letter-choirs on both sides — war magic as grinding
  artillery duel (rule 6 at scale); Vell's walls hold, barely.
- The company argues: run now (Renna) vs. finish the reading (Serel).
- Ends on: the aqueduct falls; the city's water now rationed.

### Ch 19 — The Patience of Soil  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- Under Serel's questions, Joss's first true kenning: **the patience of
  soil** — small, real, his (rule 5 made flesh). The chapter's craft job:
  make a tiny magic feel like a summit.
- Journal study: Marsh's route notes; the Scald's quiet roads mentioned.
- Ends on: Joss stills a panicking dray-team in a shell-panic — and is
  *seen* doing it (informer plant → 20).

### Ch 20 — Sold  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- The landlord sells them; snatch-squad at the Ropewalk; Mott's warning
  buys them the margin — flight to the undercity.
- Losses: gear, coin, the appointment slip; kept: Latch, journal, lives.
- Ends on: undercity dark; Bright Age stone all around, humming faintly.

### Ch 21 — Counter-Hunt  [POV: Renna] [~3,900] *(special POV #1)*
- Renna baits Sarich's net; her craft on full display; Mott redeems and
  dies covering the cut-out (first named death since Wystan — cost).
- She sees Sarich pass a Vell child begging in the cold: he gives it his
  coat *without noticing he did* (complication plant → 49, 61).
- Ends on: Renna lying to Joss about being afraid (plant → 57).

### Ch 22 — Necessary Things  [POV: Cael] [~3,700]
- Interrogations of Hall archivists; Cael takes the minutes. Nothing
  gory on page; the horror is procedure. First crack: he re-reads his
  own minutes and starts a second, true set (journal turn, plant → 36).
- Ends on: (journal) "Today we did a necessary thing. I notice I keep
  writing that."

### Ch 23 — The Standing Stair  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]
- Deep undercity: a Bright Age stair still climbing on its own; wonder
  played straight, then wrongness: a sealed Accord shrine whose serene
  imagery doesn't match the homily (first myth-crack shown, not told).
- Serel's partial heresy, spoken aloud: the Bright Lords were people.
- Ends on: Joss touching the stair rail: it *recognizes* being touched.

### Ch 24 — The Examiner  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- Serel exposed mid-crisis (a Hall survivor knows her face and rank):
  she is Vireth, an Examiner, fled — trust fracture at the worst hour.
- The fracture is about Wystan for Joss (whose fault is the guild-word
  world?), about desertion for Renna (unspoken). Serel won't beg.
- Ends on: siege horns — a wall is down somewhere. No time to be broken.

### Ch 25 — The Bought Gate  [POV: Cael] [~3,600]
- Vell's factions dicker; the Dawn buys a gate. Cael carries the purse
  and watches a city sold in an afternoon by reasonable men.
- Sarich, on the balcony, names the company's likely bolt-holes one by
  one — he has been letting them run (dread recontextualized).
- Ends on: the gate opens at moonset.

### Ch 26 — The Fall of Vell  [POV: Joss] [~4,400]
- Sack set piece: the choir broken and scattered, the Hall burning —
  archives as casualty (theme: memory dies first). The company runs the
  fire-lanes; Joss's soil-kenning holds a collapsing lane long enough.
- War's price paid on page; not grimdark, but unflinching.
- Ends on: undercity door sealed behind them; above, the city's light
  going orange.

### Ch 27 — The Cold Man's Question  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Sarich ahead of them at the undercity's mouth (he bought the gate to
  drive them down here — hunter logic). **Serel duels him**: deep
  kenning on page with hard costs; she stops short of the wick **and
  names it** (plant → 69). Frost-scars taken.
- Tovan Ninth-Cairn (stranded caravan, met ch 20 margin — promote here):
  his chant-map opens a way no map shows. Escape north.
- Ends on: Sarich, not following, asking the dark: "Was it warm, the
  thing she did? Describe it."

### Ch 28 — Occupied  [POV: Cael] [~3,500]
- Vell under the Ledger: curfew, registrations, the Hall's ashes
  cataloged. Cael processes the sack as paperwork and starts shaking.
- Assigned forward: the main expedition musters for the Scald. Sarich
  will escort. (Leash formalized.)
- Ends on: the Lector's arrival in Vell; her first on-page scene through
  Cael's awe — kindness, serenity, a pin moved on a map.

### Ch 29 — What We're For  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- North road breath chapter (scene-sequel): grief for Vell; the fracture
  mended on truer terms — Serel tells the Dawn's aim plainly: Ancarel,
  the Last Bell, a weapon to end the war and restore the age.
- Lise's first letter reaches them at a drove-inn (family thread alive).
- Mission crystallized: reach Ancarel first; deny them the Latch.
- Ends on: Renna: "So we're the ones stealing from the thieves. Good."

### Ch 30 — The Grey Country  [POV: Joss] [~3,600]
- Crossing into the Marches: emptied villages, the war's suction felt
  from its edge; first cairn sighted (Oru-Vael boundary).
- Ends on: dust behind on the Vell road — a column moving north in
  force. The Dawn doesn't hide anymore. **[Part II ends]**

### Interlude B — from the Long Count (chant excerpt, ~700)
- Oru-Vael verses: "the many" who broke the lamps; a tithe-boat that
  never came home. (Heresy seeds in the reader's ear before Part III
  says them aloud. The Count's Discord is plural.)

## Part III — The Long Road (ch 31–46)

### Ch 31 — Deserters  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Marches texture: a band of levy deserters — dangerous, then pitiable,
  then useful (news of the roads). Moral texture: the war from below.
- Renna among deserters: something unreadable (plant → 57).
- Ends on: cairn country ahead; Tovan goes quiet the closer home gets.

### Ch 32 — The Ninth Cairn  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Tovan's homecoming: the memory-debt named; the Count recites him
  *absent*. Cold welcome, warm fires; the company vouches with deeds
  (a raid on a Miswrought-taken flock turned back).
- Ends on: the memory-keepers agree to chant the quiet roads — for a
  price paid in telling (the company's true story enters the Count).

### Ch 33 — The Long Count  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- The chant heard whole at the fire: history from below — and the Count's
  Discord is *many*, and one verse mourns them. Serel goes very still.
- Craft job: exposition as ceremony with stakes (what they trade to hear it).
- Ends on: an old keeper to Serel, mildly: "Your people sing it wrong."

### Ch 34 — What False Gravings Make  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Miswrought attack on the cairn camp — horror set piece; rule 9 on page
  (a thing organized around one wrong idea, pitiable and lethal).
- Renna's craft + Joss's soil-work + the circles' rote wall = survival;
  no one wins; the Vael grieve the *thing* by its old name (it was a
  cousin, three generations back — taboo's reason made flesh).
- Ends on: Serel, quietly: "Now I will teach you what graving is, so you
  never do it."

### Ch 35 — The Three Trues  [POV: Joss] [~3,600]
- Graving theory as warning: true, yours, consistent (rule 7 taught in
  full — **the book's central Chekhov; pays ch 67**). The proverb: the
  deeper the carving, the narrower the groove.
- Joss and Renna, watch-fire honesty (their slow burn banks real heat).
- Ends on: Serel's forearms bared — frost-scars — "and I have only ever
  *stood near* the wick."

### Ch 36 — The Tuned Village  [POV: Cael] [~4,300]
- The expedition tests a recovered fragment-relic on a Marches village:
  a half-day of imposed consonance. Nothing bleeds; everyone smiles in
  unison; a grandmother sings a song she never learned and forgets her
  dead son's name. **Atrocity chapter — the Diapason's meaning at toy
  scale.** Cael's break: the true journal begins in earnest.
- Ends on: the Lector, moved to tears by the *beauty* of it.

### Ch 37 — The Hermit of the Tarn  [POV: Joss] [~4,100]
- Caul: fourfold-graven, met by his permission only; depth's cost made a
  person (map his constraint-web before drafting). What he confirms: the
  Latch treats with a *warden* — the door has a keeper (reframe: not a
  key to a weapon; a key to a conversation).
- Ends on: Caul, looking at Joss too long: "You are not deep. You are
  honest. It is rarer, and it graves cleaner."

### Ch 38 — The Price  [POV: Joss] [~3,500]
- Caul's price for counsel: **a promise from Joss** (unnamed on page;
  sequel Chekhov — log exact wording at drafting). Serel objects; Joss
  pays it anyway (his choice, his first real act of depth-adjacent will).
- Counsel given: the gate will read them; go in clean or not at all
  (plant → 57).
- Ends on: Caul stops mid-sentence, head turning: "You were followed
  cleverly."

### Ch 39 — Broken Constraints  [POV: Joss] [~4,400]
- The Dawn's snatch-team strikes the tarn (Sarich-led). Caul cannot
  fight — his web forbids — until he chooses to break it: **he shields
  the company and unravels on page.** The book's one full unraveling:
  awe and horror, rule 7's price beyond argument.
- Sarich withdraws with losses — and with a lock of ground-frost where
  Caul stood, which he pockets (log: why, decide at drafting).
- Ends on: the tarn's bowl silent; the unfindable place, findable now.

### Ch 40 — Into the Grey  [POV: Joss] [~3,500]
- Flight into the Scald's fringe: blight sensorium (wrong lichens, cold
  that maps to nothing, cairn-chant as navigation and as courage).
- Company grief for Caul processed on the move (scene-sequel in motion).
- Ends on: first mirage-memory shimmer on the horizon — a city that
  isn't there, lit and walking with people.

### Ch 41 — The Consonance Archive  [POV: Joss] [~4,400]
- A ruin the quiet roads skirt: an Accord provincial archive, its
  memory-stones still serving. **TWIST T1 ON PAGE:** the Tuning, the
  Tithe, the castes — shown in the Accord's own proud, serene records.
  The homily inverts: the paradise ran on owned minds.
- Party vertigo: Renna's rage, Tovan's verses re-heard, Serel
  vindicated-hollow (she suspected; knowing is worse), Joss re-anchored:
  *no one gets to own minds* (his purpose, stated).
- Ends on: the archive offers to *update their records* — it thinks the
  Accord still stands. Nobody answers it.

### Ch 42 — What the Lector Removed  [POV: Cael] [~3,900]
- On the march: the Lector undergoes the Dawn's third-graving rite —
  **doubt excised** — because she has read the same serene records and
  felt the same vertigo, and refuses it. (Antagonist mirrors ch 41:
  same evidence, opposite verb.)
- Ritual dread; her register changes: she no longer asks real questions.
- Ends on: Cael's journal: "She was the best of us because she doubted.
  I am watching the best of us amputate."

### Ch 43 — What Do We Do With True Things  [POV: Joss] [~3,600]
- Purpose-crisis chapter: destroy the Latch? (Can't outrun the Dawn's
  route archive; and Marsh's journal implies the door answers *force*
  eventually — a key is also the only way to *talk*.) The warden
  question posed: treat, or trust a three-thousand-year-old jailer?
- Decision: go in. Deny the Bell. Everything else is negotiable.
- Ends on: Marsh's line re-read, landing differently now: "wrong about
  the price."

### Ch 44 — Lastlight  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]
- The rim outpost: prospector culture (despised, indispensable), relic
  black market, Dawn bounty posted with their descriptions. An
  episode of betrayal-and-favor: sold to the bounty-men by one
  prospector, bought back by another for a Long-Count favor (Tovan's
  people are owed here).
- Letters from home: Lise; and Davin's one letter — the ordinary war
  (plant → 74: the armies will feel what happens).
- Ends on: quiet-road chant for the deep Scald — half the verses lost.
  "Then we walk the half we have."

### Ch 45 — The Leash Slips  [POV: Cael] [~3,800]
- Cael's attempted desertion; caught by Sarich — who lets the warning
  slip through anyway, deniably (canon: he chose to; plant → 61).
  Cael leashed tighter, marked, watched.
- His warning (prospector network, a salt-oath courier) goes ahead.
- Ends on: the Lector, mildly, without looking up: "Initiates wander.
  Note it in his file." (Post-doubt chill: it doesn't *matter* to her.)

### Ch 46 — The Land Remembers  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Into the Scald proper; Cael's warning reaches Joss (first POV-thread
  contact; the company knows the Dawn's numbers, route, and that there
  is a friend inside).
- Above the deep Scald: the stars are wrong. Nobody comments twice.
- Ends on: behind them, on the rim, the Dawn's column's fires — a city's
  worth of light, moving. **[Part III ends]**

## Part IV — The Scald (ch 47–62)

### Ch 47 — Cold-Zones  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Scald travel rules established (warmth-drinking zones where pressing
  is lethal and fires gutter; Renna's mundane cold-craft is worth more
  than kenning — theme paid in logistics).
- Ends on: first sight of a mirage-memory up close: the old war,
  replaying.

### Ch 48 — Mirage: The Bright City  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Memory-replay #1: a golden-age street, beautiful; then a Tuning
  procession — tithe-children in white, smiling in unison (T1
  reinforced shown-not-told; the beauty and the price in one frame).
- Ends on: Tovan: "The Count says: do not sing with the ghosts."

### Ch 49 — The Cold Man's Daughter  [POV: Cael] [~3,800]
- Dawn column in the Scald: attrition, cold-zone deaths, the Lector's
  serene arithmetic. Sarich humanized via Cael: the graving's story,
  the daughter alive in Sarn lands who doesn't know him (plant → 61).
- Ends on: Sarich walking a cold-zone unbothered, alone, "the only
  place he matches the weather."

### Ch 50 — The Shepherd  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Miswrought territory: the Shepherd — a Miswrought that *collects*
  travelers (organized around a false understanding of "keeping safe").
  Renna taken; rescue by craft: Joss's soil-patience used clever and
  small against a titanic wrongness (no power-up; wits and rules).
- Ends on: the Shepherd's flock, released, not all of them recent.

### Ch 51 — Mirage: The Many  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]
- Memory-replay #2 — **TWIST T2 PART A ON PAGE:** the Discord as crowds,
  as people: a Tuning temple broken open with joy; then tribunal
  scaffolds in the same square, a purge feeding on itself. The party
  argues over what they're watching (liberation? terror? both — the
  book's honest answer).
- Ends on: Serel: "Both true. Write both down." (Cael echo, unknowing.)

### Ch 52 — The Refuge  [POV: Cael] [~4,100]
- The order Cael can't file away: a prospector refuge "requisitioned"
  for the column's survival, cold-zone-marched. **His real defection:**
  he burns his leash, takes the true journal, and runs for the enemy
  he believes in now.
- Ends on: Sarich watching him go — and reporting the direction wrong
  by one degree (deniable mercy #2; plant → 61).

### Ch 53 — The Archivist's Confession  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Cael reaches the company; tense integration — and Serel knows him:
  her former student (the door she left open in his mind). His journal
  read aloud at the fire: the tuned village, the Lector's graving,
  the column's strength. Trust built on documents and Renna's knife
  visible on the table.
- Ends on: Joss and Cael's first real exchange — mirror recognized.

### Ch 54 — The Sheer  [POV: Joss] [~3,800]
- The glass sea: awe chapter; Ancarel below, lit, waiting, vast — the
  first *intact* golden-age thing they've seen, and it's a prison.
- Finding the gate: the Latch grows colder toward it (compass plant
  from ch 16 pays).
- Ends on: a voice in every skull at once, courteous: the Warden.

### Ch 55 — The Warden's Half-Truths  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]
- Ilvane via dream-contact: tests dressed as conversation. **T2 PART B:**
  she says "we" of the Discord and of the Accord's end both — and won't
  yet say which side she means, because the answer is both (the Truce
  named, not yet explained).
- The company negotiates entry: the gate has a price (unnamed → 57).
- Ends on: "Come to the door. Bring the key. Bring nothing false."

### Ch 56 — The Door Under the Glass  [POV: Joss] [~4,300]
- The Dawn arrives early (Lastlight betrayal — the bounty prospector).
  Running fight across the Sheer to the gate; Tovan maimed (two
  fingers; keeps the joke); the company reaches the descent with the
  column's van behind.
- Ends on: the gate: no lock, no handle — a threshold that *reads*.

### Ch 57 — The Reading  [POV: Joss] [~4,400]
- **The gate's price: be understood.** Character crucible: Renna's
  desertion (ch 8) spoken; Serel's founding of the Dawn's project
  spoken (her whole truth); Cael's unrenounced utopia spoken; Joss's
  wanting-to-matter and Wystan-guilt spoken — he passes *clean*, and
  the gate's regard lingers on him (plant → 67, Ilvane's interest).
- The crucible mends the company at a truer depth (no cheap absolution;
  the sayings cost).
- Ends on: the door opens on lit streets and Ilvane in warden's grey:
  "Four honest people. It has been some time."

### Ch 58 — The Shut City  [POV: Joss] [~4,000]

- Ilvane in person: courtesy, arithmetic, three millennia of alone.
- Ends on: thunder above — the Dawn has reached the gate and begun
  to knock.

### Interlude C — A letter, Truce-era (~1,100)

### Ch 59 — The Knock  [POV: Cael] [~3,800]

- Ends on: the first seal-stone in the gate cracking, and the city's

### Ch 60 — The Warden's Bargain  [POV: Joss] [~4,100]
- Ilvane lays the truth out (§5.5 revealed): **the Bell keeps the
  seals.** The Dawn's breach is already straining them; there is no

- Ends on: Ilvane, relieved beyond bearing, begins to say what

### Ch 61 — The Thief's Way  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]

## Part V — The Last Bell (ch 63–76)

### Ch 63 — Descent  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Into inner Ancarel toward the Bell; the city waking in rings around
  the intrusion; Ilvane holding doors with visible strain (her power
  implied, not displayed).
- Ends on: the Diapason chamber's antechamber — and the sound, felt
  in the teeth, of something enormous *almost* ringing.

### Ch 64 — Heirs  [POV: Cael] [~3,900]
- With the Lector's van in the Sleeper galleries: the Dawn meets its
  dream in the flesh — sealed Archons, faces serene. The Lector speaks
  to one through the seal: "We are your heirs." No answer. Yet.
- Dawn casualties to vault defenses tallied in her serene arithmetic.
- Ends on: her hand on the chamber door; the antechamber breached.

### Ch 65 — Locks That Read  [POV: Joss] [~4,100]
- The vault trials: understanding-locks — doors that open only to true
  statements about oneself, spoken aloud (crucible again, deeper; the
  system's theme literalized as architecture).
- Company beats under pressure; Ilvane passes a door with a statement
  three thousand years rehearsed (do not reveal which — sequels).
- Ends on: the heart antechamber; the Bell visible at last (describe:
  a held choir, not a machine — ten thousand preserved minds).

### Ch 66 — What Denial Requires  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Serel's counsel chapter: the choice mapped one last time (ring =
  tuned world; deny = broken seals; abstain = the Dawn rings it).
  Ilvane's testimony completes T2/Truce; her plain answer given: she
  will take the breaking over the ringing.
- **Denial's mechanism:** the Bell's heart must be unkeyed by a mind
  the Bell cannot tune — or it re-tunes the unkeyer mid-act. The Three
  Trues checked against Joss (ch 35 pays): he volunteers.
- Ends on: Serel, to Joss, examiner to the last: "Then let us verify
  your understanding, one final time."

### Ch 67 — The Graving  [POV: Joss] [~4,400]
- **Joss's first graving: rootedness.** His whole book of small
  honesties is the material; near-unravel on the dodged Wystan-guilt
  (the knot from ch 8) — passes by accepting it *true*, not absolved.
  The thematic summit chapter; every rule honored; modest result,
  total stakes.
- Ends on: frost melting off him; the chamber doors failing; the
  Lector through.

### Ch 68 — The Grand Tuning  [POV: Cael] [~4,000]
- The Lector takes the chamber and begins: the Bell gathering toward
  one continent-mending chord (keep scale claims within §5.5 —
  city-scale direct, and *that is enough to end the war*). Cael inside
  the tuning field: his mind gently corrected in real time; he anchors
  on the journal's recorded truths (self-knowledge as resistance —
  system-consistent, at mortal strain).
- Ends on: through the wrongness, he sees Serel walk in alone.

### Ch 69 — The Argument, Ended  [POV: Joss, at the threshold] [~4,300]
- **Serel vs. the Lector:** thirty years of argument as a duel of deep
  kennings — exactness against certainty, with hard rules and visible
  costs. Serel cannot win; she can *spend*: she presses to the wick
  (ch 27's plant pays), buying the walk. Dies as chosen, unforgetting
  at last; the still hand steady to the end.
- Ends on: the corridor to the heart, open. The bell-sound rising.

### Ch 70 — The Walk  [POV: Joss] [~4,200]
- Joss in the tuning field: rooted, ungrippable, walking. Intercut:
  Renna, Cael, Ilvane holding the door. The Lector face to face:
  she *cannot comprehend* the refusal (post-42 flaw pays); her every
  lever finds no purchase on a mind that knows itself.
- He unkeys the heart.
- Ends on: the chord collapsing into — silence. Then the other sound:
  everywhere, stone splitting. The seals.

### Ch 71 — The Tuner, Tuned  [POV: Cael] [~3,900]
- The Silence-echo cascades; the galleries wake. **The Archon ("the
  Serene") wakes first**, regards the Lector and her project — finds
  it quaint — and *tunes her*, gently, mid-sentence: her god answers,
  and she becomes its instrument (fate per her file; horror beat, not
  gore).
- Cael, witness, does the only thing he is: he writes it down.
- Ends on: the Archon noticing Cael's watching — and *approving* of
  witnesses.

### Ch 72 — The Waking City  [POV: Joss] [~4,300]
- Flight through cascade: the unsealed in one-beat glimpses — the
  Serene's terrible reasonableness; the Firebrand's first question
  ("Did we hold the tribunals?" — no safe answer); the Chorus stirring
  below, not loose (book 2's dread, seeded and left).
- Ilvane's corridor holds; the company out under the Sheer.
- Ends on: behind them, Ancarel fully lit for the first time in three
  thousand years — like a city celebrating.

### Ch 73 — The Scald Sings  [POV: Joss] [~3,600]
- Out on the glass: survivors' count; grief chapter (Serel foremost;
  Caul and Mott and Wystan in the ledger of it). The Scald's
  mirage-memories are gone — and the land makes a sound no one living
  has heard: a false spring. Not comfort; change.
- Ends on: Ilvane on the Sheer at dawn, counting the woken aloud.

### Ch 74 — Witnesses  [POV: Joss] [~3,900]
- Return leg to Lastlight and the cairn country: the world already
  shaking — the armies *felt* the echo (Davin's thread pays); the war
  stuttering on its assumptions; rumor outrunning truth wearing myth's
  old clothes ("the Discord is loose").
- Cael's decision: publish — testimony against myth, whatever it costs
  house Immra. Tovan pays his memory-debt: tells the whole tale into
  the Long Count. Lise's letter answered honestly, at last.
- Ends on: Renna, to Joss, about the road ahead: "You'll want company."

### Ch 75 — The Warden's Count  [POV: Joss] [~3,700]
- Ilvane's departure and warning: the Truce is everyone's problem now;
  she goes "to count who woke" (sequel hinge armed). What remains of
  the Dawn: cells with a martyr-story and no Lector (log sequel
  posture). The Serene's first public act arrives as garbled news —
  wrong, of course, in the old familiar way (myth begins again, on
  page).
- Ends on: the company's shape resolved: Joss once-graven, Renna
  beside him, Cael's manuscript, roads diverging with intent to
  reconverge.

### Ch 76 — Coda: The Year  [POV: Joss] [~3,200]
- The wold, earliest spring: Fennstead's kin-farm; Bram's silence,
  Lise's arithmetic, Petra's letter-quick questions (sequel seed
  touched). Joss home and not-home; the good knife returned to the
  table it came from.
- Final image: on a road somewhere in the world, something ancient and
  courteous stops a farmer and asks what year it is — and thanks them,
  and walks on.
- **[Book one ends.]**
