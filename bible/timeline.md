## Calendar system

- Scholarly reckoning: **A.S.** (After Silence). Year 0 = the Silence.
- Harthen: **Charter Years** (CY). CY 1 = A.S. 2363. **Story opens CY 611 =
  A.S. 2974, late summer (harvest).**
- Twelve months, four seasons, as familiar; month names surface in prose as
  needed and get glossary entries when they do.

### Planned seasonal arc (for outline consistency)

- Part I: late summer–early autumn 2974 (harvest; the wold).
- Part II: autumn; Vell falls as first frosts come.
- Part III: winter on the Marches and cairn country (cold worsens
  warmth-costs — thematic and tactical).
- Part IV: deep winter into false thaw; the Scald.
- Part V: earliest spring 2975; "the Scald sings" as false spring. Total
  span ≈ 8 months.
