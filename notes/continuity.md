## World state

- **The war (as of ch 3):** two years old; now felt in Harrowmere as prices,
  requisitions, road traffic, levy writs, billeted refugees, the burned
  fodder-yards at the Ferrand toll (cause never established — rumors run
  carelessness / Ascendancy agents / the Discord), and now the horses gone.
  **Fire's consequence:** the toll can't winter remounts, so the levy's
  horse-trains make up early and south at Marle — marked beasts called in
  district-wide. Harthen levies garrison Concord walls under the treaty
  (folk understanding). Courier-word via Marley Chessom all summer: "the
  Seam holds" — contradicted privately by Renna (worse than the couriers
  say; unreported lost towns).
- **Requisition:** the crown's fifth collected; **horse requisition executed
  ch 3**: nine parish beasts (Fenn gelding, Aldery dray mare, Carter's road
  pair, a Chessom riding cob, +5) joined a district string of thirty-odd,
  gone east over the toll to Ferrand, then south to Marle. Chits at the
  crown's price. Serjeant Cropp's party moved on with the drove; Renna Pike
  (shirelevy scout) stays billeted at the reeve's until the trains go south
  at month's end.
- **Harrowmere minor state:** hidden stock the crown never found: a mare in
  the marl-pit shaw, a red heifer in the alder carr (Renna knows, didn't
  report). Seth Hodge (warrener, parish's best shot) lost a public shilling
  wager to Renna (sentinel rook, eleven paces, from inside a windrow); the
  rook went to Gret Dunmow. Old levy butts on the green, grass-grown
  (statute shooting long lapsed). Rookery elms stand behind the mill.
- **Season:** early autumn; mists on the mere, dew heavy, stubble awaiting
  the plow. Ch 3 spans Inning + 8–9 days; levy report-day is Inning + 51
  (42 days remain).
