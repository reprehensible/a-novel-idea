# Chapter Summaries — The Compressed Novel

> 150–250 words per chapter, added immediately after drafting. This file IS
> the novel's memory: it must stay small enough to read in full before every
> drafting session (~80 chapters × 200 words ≈ 16k words — affordable).
>
> A good summary records what a future writing session needs: events, choices,
> reveals (who learned what), state changes (injuries, items gained/lost,
> locations), emotional beats between characters, and any plants/payoffs.
> Not prose quality — information density.

## Format

```markdown
### Ch NNN — Title  [POV: name]  [words: N,NNN]  [in-world date: —]
Summary...
**State changes:** ...
**Plants/payoffs:** P00X planted / P00Y paid.
```

### Ch 001 — Harvest  [POV: Joss]  [words: ~3,640]  [in-world date: late summer, CY 611 / A.S. 2974]
Harvest's end at Harrowmere. Crown purveyors tally the village's grain (the
crown's fifth, heavier than last year); Joss assists Wystan Crake renewing the
granary's keeping-letters — rote magic and its warmth-cost (fogged breath in
summer) established on page. Family established: Bram (taciturn), Lise
(practical spine), Davin (lucky, careless-edged), Petra (letter-quick; catches
the purveyor's tally error, is overruled — "It is if it's written"). The
Fenns' young gelding is chalk-marked for assessment. At the Inning
(harvest-home feast in the moot-barn), Widow Aldery says the Stillfaith grace;
Wystan tells the Bright Lords / Discord lamp-story (myth seeded in folk form).
Mid-feast, levy writs arrive: three conscriptions — Col Aldery, Edd Dunmow,
and Davin, to report at Ferrand in 51 days. Lise gives Joss pie and marching
arithmetic. Near dawn, Joss and Davin, on the barn roof, watch an unexplained
fire burning on the war road past the Ferrand toll — where nothing should
burn — and riders' lanterns moving fast in the dark.
**State changes:** Davin conscripted (reports in 51 days); gelding marked for
requisition; war has visibly reached the district's edge.
**Plants/payoffs:** P018 planted (lamp-story myth); P019 planted (Davin/the
ordinary war); P020 planted (Petra's exactness); gelding-marking sets up the
ch 3 horse-taking; spring memory of a twelve-kenner choir seeds rule-6 texture.
