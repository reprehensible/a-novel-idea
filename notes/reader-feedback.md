# Test-Reader Feedback

> Reports from fresh-context "test reader" subagent panels, archived verbatim
> (lightly trimmed only for length). **Treat as feedback, not as change
> requests** (author's rule): impressions inform revision judgment; they are
> not a to-do list. Decisions taken *because of* feedback get logged in
> `decisions-log.md` like any other decision.

## Panel protocol (established 2026-07-31)

Three personas, run with fresh context, each reading ONLY the manuscript
prose under review (never the bible/outline — readers stay unspoiled):

1. **The Enthusiast** — genre superfan; wants lore, systems, action,
   momentum, character beats; theorizes.
2. **The Literary Reader** — skeptical of genre habit, open to its best;
   judges sentences, POV discipline, thematic substance, earned emotion.
3. **The AI Skeptic** — genre-fluent; radar for machine-feel: stock
   phrasing, over-signaling, uniformity, airbrushed smoothness; quotes
   exact trip-wires; gives a DNF risk.

Readers report in character, impressions-not-rewrites. Panels run at the
author's request (not automatically per chapter).

## Panel 1 — ch 001 "Harvest" (2026-07-31)

### Reader 1 — the Enthusiast

Verdict: would keep reading ("went looking for chapter two"). Highlights:

- The worn on-ramp (quiet village, then the writs) works "because you keep
  paying the reader in specifics"; the fourth-verse joke paying off inside
  one chapter is where he decided to trust the book.
- Magic: clocked the letters' mechanics correctly (fading like an egg going
  stale, touch-detection, warmth-cost — "the fogging-breath-in-a-warm-loft
  detail is fantastic"); clocked the hedge-tier vs. military-choir-tier
  split; "like a hive in the wood" praised. **Ask: one more concrete rule
  or limit within the next few chapters** — "I'm theorizing on fumes and I
  want to theorize on data." (Ch 2 already does this by design.)
- Lore: doesn't believe the lamp-story is just a story (as intended);
  caught the unexplained "war road" name; caught the chalk motif
  (tallies/assessment cross/letters) and read it as "writing as power —
  'it is if it's written'" (motif was partly deliberate, now canon: lean in).
- Characters: Joss, Davin, Wystan, Lise land ("she would not give the shire
  the satisfaction" — one line, whole woman). Bram "a closed door... he'll
  need a crack eventually." **Petra "is a function so far"** — assumed
  she'd get her chapter (she won't in bk 1; watch that her plants stay
  scenes, not signals).
- Pacing "close to ideal" for a 290k opener; ending curtain line praised.
- Theories: (1) Joss has the actual gift — evidence: touch/hands motif,
  Wystan's unsaid thing (correct); (2) choirs are weaponized letters and
  the fire means the enemy has an answer to them (half right, usefully
  wrong); (3) "That gelding is coming back. 'Written down together' —
  Davin said it as a joke; the book didn't." (Correct; ch 3.)

### Reader 2 — the Literary Reader

Verdict: would read on — "the chapter appears to know what it's about."

- Prose: praised "agricultural wit" — metaphors native to the POV's world
  ("agreed with it beforehand," "lucky the way some men are tall," "the
  wold's way of shouting," "somebody else's arithmetic being done on his
  family's winter" — "the whole chapter in miniature"). "We are quiet /
  said the barn, and then was anything but" — culture "used rather than
  invented."
- **Stumbles by reaching:** (1) "somehow one instrument" *explains* the
  held-breath image and is the weaker sentence; (2) the leaning-tree
  figure returns in the final paragraph spelled out — the closing
  "which was that... and that... and that" tells what the roof silence
  already told; "everything between 'Aye' and [the last two sentences] is
  the author not quite trusting them." (Note: valid; the final paragraph
  over-explains. Candidate trim in revision; logged for the Phase 3 pass.)
- POV: caught "That was the thing Joss fixed on, after" as a retrospective
  narrator flicker — "you only get to have him on purpose." (Decision
  needed: adopt the faint retrospective narrator as deliberate, sparingly,
  or cut. See decisions-log.)
- Characters as people: Bram's "It is if it's written" = "a whole
  philosophy of survival in five words... also what the book is about";
  Petra "a character and a thesis in one sentence"; Edd's "forever" =
  "grief pre-paid." **Davin: "a type beautifully rendered; he isn't yet a
  person who could surprise me."** (Fair; his ch 4–8 beats and the roof
  scene in ch 7 should complicate him before he exits.)
- Theme: read the chapter as "a meditation on inscription as power...
  Even the magic is literacy. Kafka in a barley field." (This is the book;
  reader found the spine unprompted.)
- Genre machinery: the fireside myth is where "the series bible [breathes]
  behind the chestnut" — saved by the framing, but "I'll need the book to
  complicate it, not deliver it." (It will; T1/T2.)
- Sentimentality: pie scene earns it; **the gelding "unwritten-down in his
  own opinion" is "one beat past earned, into charming."** (Noted as a
  tic-risk: whimsy-anthropomorphism under emotional load.)
- Parting instruction: the burning horizon kept "modest" — "Keep that
  modesty. It's your best instinct."

### Reader 3 — the AI Skeptic

Verdict: **"reads as authored. High confidence — 85–90%"**; DNF risk for
the chapter ~10% ("bounces off the deliberate slowness or the density of
ruralisms, not off machine-smell").

- Convinced by: odd specificity doing work ("somebody else's arithmetic,"
  magic introduced through *maintenance*, "hot mash in harvest weather...
  winter coat" — "observation, not generation"); the forty-one/forty-three
  exchange ("bureaucratic fatalism compressed into nine words"); the
  fourth-verse callback ("Machines don't do callback-as-gallows-humor that
  lands that dry"); five family members with five distinct registers.
- **Pings (all mild):**
  1. "like a held breath" (the choir simile) — "the one borrowed image in
     the chapter... off the rack." (Note: the held-note/choir resonance was
     deliberate, but the reader's right that it reads as stock *here*
     because the music-vocabulary layer isn't visible yet. Phase 3
     candidate: replace or delay the image until choir vocabulary is
     established.)
  2. **The aphorism cadence** — closing-button aphorisms arriving ~once
     per paragraph in stretches: "One chapter in, it reads as voice.
     Twenty chapters in... it'll start reading as manufacture." Leaning-tree
     image: "quota spent on it for a while."
  3. "and knew better" skirts over-signaling (earned back by Lise's beat).
- Texture: "the paperwork has weight... the chalk cross on the horse
  frightened me more than the fire on the horizon, which I suspect is the
  point." (It is.)
- Forward worry: aphorism tic calcifying; "wry-omniscient warmth
  flattening every character into the same knowingness."

### Synthesis (what the process takes forward — logged in decisions-log)

All three would keep reading. Strong convergence on: distinct dialogue
registers, warmth-cost eeriness, administrative dread, the inscription
theme (two readers found it unprompted — it's legible), and the
fourth-verse callback as the chapter's trust-winning move.

Convergent cautions, adopted as **drafting watch-list** (not retroactive
edits, per the author's feedback-not-change-requests rule):
1. **Aphorism rationing** (Skeptic §2 + Literary "one clause too pleased"):
   vary where the polished line lands; not every paragraph gets a button;
   some paragraphs must end plain. Added to ban-list watch items.
2. **Whimsy under emotional load** (Literary's gelding note): anthropo-
   morphizing charm near grief beats — use sparingly.
3. **Trust the image once** (both): don't explain a simile ("somehow one
   instrument"); don't respell a figure at chapter's end (leaning-tree
   closing). Final-paragraph over-explanation of ch 1 = Phase 3 trim
   candidate; "held breath" simile = Phase 3 replace/delay candidate.
4. **Davin** needs a beat that surprises before he exits (ch 4–8 room).
5. **Petra's plants stay scenes, not signals** (Enthusiast: "a function so
   far").
6. Retrospective-narrator flicker ("fixed on, after"): ADOPTED as
   deliberate device, rationed (see decisions-log).
